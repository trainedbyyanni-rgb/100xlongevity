One on One
Coaching

Eight weeks of direct work on training, cognitive performance, and compound protocols, built around your biology rather than a template.

Most coaching in this space is a training program with a meal plan attached. That is not what this is.

The work here starts one layer underneath the program itself. Your recovery capacity, your sleep architecture, your baseline neurochemical environment, and your actual response to training stimulus determine whether any program produces results. Two people running identical protocols produce different outcomes because those underlying variables are different, and almost nobody accounts for that before writing the plan.

Over eight weeks we identify what is actually limiting you, build the protocol around that, and adjust it as your response data comes in.

What The Eight Weeks Cover

01

Training structure built around your recovery capacity, schedule, and biomechanics rather than a split you found online. This includes exercise selection based on how your structure actually loads the target muscle, not what works for everyone else.

02

Cognitive performance protocol covering sleep architecture, stimulant use if applicable, and the compound decisions that support rather than deplete the systems producing your output.

03

Compound and protocol guidance grounded in mechanism. What is worth running, what is not, what the actual dosing logic is, and what the tradeoffs are. This is research education, not prescription.

04

Direct access for questions as they come up, plus a weekly check in where we review what changed and adjust.

Who This Is Not For

Not For

This is not for someone looking for a meal plan and a workout PDF. Those exist for far less money and they work fine for people who want them.

Not For

This is not for someone who wants to be told what to do without understanding why. Half the value here is that you leave able to make these decisions yourself.

Not For

This is not for someone unwilling to address sleep and recovery first. Every protocol is downstream of those two variables and there is no compound that compensates for ignoring them.
